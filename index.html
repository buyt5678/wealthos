KNOW BEFORE YOU GROW

Investing basics

▦

What is an ETF?

An exchange-traded fund holds a basket of investments. It can provide diversification, but its value can still fall.

◉

Why diversify?

Spreading investments can reduce dependence on one company or asset. It does not remove market risk.

↗

Compound growth

Returns can earn returns over time. Compounding also works in reverse when investments lose value.

⌁

Risk and time horizon

Money needed soon generally belongs in lower-volatility, accessible places rather than stock investments.
